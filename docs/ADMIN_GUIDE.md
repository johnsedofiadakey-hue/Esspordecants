# Esspor admin guide

This guide shows you how to run the shop on your own: add fragrances, set prices and stock, handle orders, and change what customers see. You can read it on your phone, inside the admin (open **Help**), or print it.

> **Tip:** Nothing you do in the admin can break the shop. New products are **drafts** that customers cannot see until you press **Publish**, and you can always take a product off the shop again.

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the Esspor website and scroll to the very bottom. Under the small print there is a faint link called **Staff login**. Tap it. (You can also save the admin address, https://esspordecants-admin.web.app, as a bookmark.)
2. Type your **email** and **password**, then tap **Sign in**.
3. The first time, change the temporary password you were given: open **Settings** (on a phone: **More**, then **Settings**) and fill in **Your password**. Use at least 12 characters, for example three or four words that mean something to you.

**Get around.** On a phone, the bar at the bottom has **Overview**, **Products**, **Orders**, **Content** and **More**. **More** holds **Settings**, **Help**, the new-order alert switch, and **Sign out**. On a computer the same items are in the menu on the left.

**Put it on your phone's home screen.** On an iPhone, open the admin in Safari, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**. On Android, open the browser menu and choose **Add to Home screen**. You then open the admin like an app.

> **Tip:** On a shared phone or computer, always tap **Sign out** when you finish. Never share your password.

## 2. Add a fragrance

Go to **Products** and tap **Add a fragrance**. You fill in one page, from top to bottom. The row of small circles near the top (Fragrance, Scent, Sizes, Details, Receives) shows how much is done and jumps to each part when tapped. Your answers are saved on your phone as you type, so if you are interrupted you can come back and carry on.

### The fragrance
- **Fragrance house** is the brand on the original bottle, for example Lattafa or Dior. Start typing and your earlier houses are offered, so spelling stays the same.
- **Fragrance name** is the name of the scent.
- **Strength** is printed on the bottle: Eau de Parfum, Eau de Toilette and so on. Tap one. If yours is not listed, type it in the **Other** box and press Enter.
- **Who is it for?** Tap **For her**, **For him** or **For anyone**. Customers can filter the shop by this.

### The scent
- **Scent family** is the main character of the fragrance, such as Woody, Floral or Oud. Tap one.
- **Flavours** are everything that describes how it smells, such as vanilla, spicy, sweet or oud. Tap as many as fit (up to 12). Type your own in **Add your own** and press Enter. These become the tags on the product page and the **Flavours** filter in the shop.
- **Top, Heart and Base notes** are the notes the perfume house lists. Type one and press Enter, and it turns into a tag; tap the **×** on a tag to remove it. Suggestions appear as you type. **Only list notes you are sure of.**

### Bottle sizes and prices
1. Tap every size you sell this fragrance in: **2 ml**, **3 ml**, **5 ml**, **10 ml**, and so on. Use the **Other ml** box for a size that is not shown.
2. For each size that appears, fill in:
   - **Price (GH₵)**: what the customer pays for that size.
   - **In stock**: how many bottles of that size you have ready.
   - **Code (SKU)**: made for you (for example LAT-KHA-5). You only change it if you have your own system.
3. Tap **Remove** on a size to take it off.

### Details customers read
- **One-line summary** shows on the shop cards (up to 280 characters).
- **Full description** tells the story: the mood, the scent, when to wear it.
- **How long does it last?** and **How far does it travel?** Tap the dots from 1 to 5. Tap the same dot again to clear it.
- **Best seasons** and **Best for** (for example Evenings or Date night) help customers choose.
- **Feature on the home page** puts the fragrance in the highlighted row on the home page.

### What the customer receives
- **Original brand** is the brand of the bottle you fill from.
- **Authenticity statement**, for example: "Decanted by hand from an authentic, sealed retail bottle." **Only write what is true.**
- **What arrives**, for example: "One 5 ml glass decant with a fine-mist sprayer, labelled and sealed."
- **Bottle material**, **Cap or sprayer** and **Label version** describe the little bottle you send.
- Tick **I have checked the wording** when you have read everything through.

> **Tip:** After your first fragrance, these wording and bottle boxes are filled in for you on the next one. Check they are right for that fragrance.

### Save
Tap **Save draft and add photos** at the bottom. The fragrance and all its sizes are saved as a **draft**, and the product page opens so you can add photos. **Nothing is on the shop yet.**

## 3. Add photos and videos, then publish

On the product page, the **Publish checklist** at the top shows what is still missing (for example "9 of 11 done"). Tap it to see the list. The **Publish** button stays greyed out until every item is done.

### Photos and videos
1. Scroll to **Photos and videos**.
2. Choose a **Role**. The first photo should be **Decant · front**: the real little bottle the customer will receive. The other roles are **Decant · side**, **Cap / sprayer**, **Packaging**, **Source bottle** (the original full-size bottle, shown as a reference) and **Editorial** (mood pictures).
3. Tap **Choose files** and pick one or many photos or short videos from your phone.
4. Tick **These are real photographs or videos of the item, not renders or concept media**, then tap **Upload**.
5. Under each uploaded picture, fill in the **Alt text** (a short description such as "Actual 5 ml Esspor decant, front view"). For a video, describe the video. For a **Source bottle** picture, the description must say it is the original bottle.
6. Tick **Real photo** (or **Real footage**) and **Approved for storefront**. Only approved pictures show on the shop.
7. Use the **←** and **→** arrows to change the order. Delete removes a picture.

**Good to know.** Photos are made smaller automatically, so you can use photos straight from your phone. Videos can be up to 20 MB, so keep them short (about 15 seconds). A product can have up to 16 photos and 4 videos. **Never use computer-made or concept images as product photos.**

### Publish
When the checklist shows **Ready to publish**, tap **Publish**. The fragrance is now on the shop. To remove it, open it again and tap **Unpublish**: it goes back to a draft. **Archive** puts it away without deleting it, and **Restore** brings it back as a draft.

> **Tip:** A published product is checked every time you save. If an edit would leave it incomplete, for example deleting the description, the edit is refused and the product stays as it was.

## 4. Change prices, sizes and stock

Open the product and scroll to **Sizes, prices and stock**.

- **Change a price:** edit the **Price (GHS)** box on that size, then tap anywhere else. It saves by itself.
- **Add a size:** tap one of the size buttons under **Add a size**, fill in **Price (GHS)** and **Opening stock**, then tap **Add size**.
- **Add or remove stock:** tap **Adjust stock** on a size. In **Change**, type a plus number to add (for example 10) or a minus number to remove (for example -2). In **Reason**, write why ("New batch decanted"). Tap **Apply**. Every change is recorded.
- **Stop selling a size for now:** untick **On sale**. Tick it again to bring it back. (A size that has been ordered before cannot be deleted, so use this instead.)
- **Different-looking bottle for one size:** tick **Different bottle**, then pick its photo in **Size photo**, so the shop shows what really arrives.
- **Low-stock alert at:** when stock falls to this number the shop shows "Few left" and the **Overview** page lists it.

Stock goes down by itself when someone places an order, and comes back by itself if the order is cancelled before it is sent.

## 5. Add many fragrances from a spreadsheet

Useful when you start with a long list.

1. **Products**, then **Import from spreadsheet**.
2. Tap **Download template** and open it in Excel or Google Sheets.
3. Fill it in with **one row per size**. Rows with the same house and name become one product with several sizes. Columns: house, name, concentration, family, **audience** (women, men or unisex), **flavours** (separate with semicolons), short description, description, top, heart and base notes, source brand, **sku** (a code for each size, letters, numbers and hyphens), **size_ml**, **price_ghs**, **stock**.
4. Save as **CSV** (in Excel: Save as, CSV; in Google Sheets: File, Download, CSV).
5. Tap **Choose file**, then **Check the file**. Fix anything it lists (it names the row), save, and check again.
6. Tap **Import … as drafts**.

Imported fragrances are always drafts and never overwrite one you already have. Open each to add photos, the "What the customer receives" details, and then publish it.

## 6. Handle orders

Go to **Orders**. A number beside **Orders** (and on the browser tab) shows how many need action. Tap an order to open it.

### What each status means
| Status | What the customer sees | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Pending payment | "Waiting for payment" | Nothing. For online payments, if they do not pay within 45 minutes the order is cancelled and the stock returns. |
| Paid | "Payment received" | Prepare the order, then tap **Mark processing**. |
| Processing | "Preparing your order" | When it leaves you, tap **Mark shipped**. |
| Shipped | "On its way" (delivery) or "Ready for pickup" | After it arrives or is collected, tap **Mark delivered**. |
| Delivered | "Delivered" or "Collected" | Done. |
| Cancelled / Refunded | "Cancelled" / "Refunded" | None. |

### On an order
- **Customer:** the name, phone number (tap to call), whether it is **Delivery** or **Pickup**, and the address. **Message on WhatsApp** opens a chat with them.
- **Note for the customer:** customers get **no** SMS, email or WhatsApp messages from the shop, so use this note to tell them things: the rider's name and number, an expected time, pickup instructions. It appears on their **Track order** page. **Never put anything private here.**
- **Internal notes:** only you and your team see these.
- **Print packing slip** prints the order.
- **Update status:** the buttons shown are the only moves allowed from the current status. The **Note** box saves with the change.

### Customers and order numbers
After ordering, customers are told to take a screenshot of their order number (for example ESS-1042-7KQ2). They type it into **Track order** on the website to see the status and any note you wrote. If a customer loses the number, search for them on the **Orders** page by **name or phone number**.

### Cancel or refund
- **Mark cancelled** is for an order that will not be sent. If it was not yet sent, the stock goes back on the shelf automatically.
- **Mark refunded** only records the refund in the admin. **It does not send money back.** Do the refund in your Paystack dashboard, then mark the order refunded.
- A red warning "A payment arrived after this order was cancelled" means a customer paid just after the 45-minute limit. Refund them in Paystack, or contact them and take the order by hand.

### Export
**Export to spreadsheet** on the **Orders** page downloads your orders as a file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.

### New-order alerts
Open **More** and switch on **Chime and pop up for new orders**. While the admin is open on that phone or computer, it checks every minute and plays a chime when an order needs you. It does not work when the admin is closed, so check **Orders** at set times too.

## 7. Delivery and pickup

Customers choose between the options you set up here, so at least one must be switched on for checkout to work. Go to **Settings**, then **Delivery options** (owners only).

1. Under the list, fill in **Code** (lowercase, letters, numbers and hyphens, for example accra-courier), **Label** (what customers see, for example "Courier delivery"), **Type** (**Delivery** asks the customer for an address; **Pickup** does not) and **Price (GHS)** (0 for free).
2. Tap **Add (inactive)**.
3. Tick **Active** on that row to turn it on. Untick it to turn it off.

You can change a price in its row at any time. Customers see the fee before they pay.

## 8. Store details, banner and pages

### Store details (owners)
**Settings**, then **Store details**:
- **Top banner** is the strip above the menu, for example "Order before 8PM for next-day delivery | Mon–Fri". Leave it empty to hide it. **Keep it true: it is a promise to customers.**
- **WhatsApp number**, **Phone number**, **Email** and **Instagram** appear in the footer and on the Contact page. The WhatsApp number powers the "Chat on WhatsApp" buttons.
- **Pickup address and hours** appear on the Delivery & returns and Contact pages.

Tap **Save details**. Customers see the changes within a few minutes.

### Content (home page text and information pages)
**Content** lets you change words on the shop without a developer.
1. Type a short **slug** (lowercase words with hyphens), choose a **Placement**, and tap **Create**.
2. Fill in **Eyebrow** (the small line above), **Title**, **Body** (blank lines make new paragraphs), and optionally a button (**Button label** and **Button link**) and an image.
3. Tap **Publish**.

Useful placements: **home-hero** replaces the big message at the top of the home page, and **home-story** replaces the short intro below it. To rewrite the **Privacy**, **Terms** or **Delivery & returns** pages, create an entry with placement **page** and the slug **privacy**, **terms** or **delivery-returns**. Until you publish one, the shop shows its standard wording. **Have someone read those pages to make sure they suit your business.**

## 9. Your team and password

- **Change your password:** **Settings**, **Your password**, then **Change password**.
- **Add someone (owners only):** **Settings**, **Team**. Enter **Name**, **Email**, **Role** and a **Temporary password** (at least 12 characters), then tap **Add admin**. Tell them the password privately and ask them to change it.
- **Roles:** an **owner** can do everything, including the team, delivery options and store details. An **editor** can manage products, content and orders.
- In the **Team** list you can change a role, tap **Reset password** (they are signed out everywhere) or **Disable** someone so they can no longer sign in. **Enable** brings them back.
- **Recent activity** (owners) lists who changed what.

## 10. Every day and every week

**Every day**
1. Open **Orders**. Prepare the **Paid** ones and move them along with the status buttons.
2. Add a **Note for the customer** where it helps (rider, time).
3. Look at **Overview** for items that are **Low** or **Out of stock** and add stock where needed.

**Every week**
1. Add new fragrances and retire ones you no longer sell (**Unpublish** or turn off **On sale**).
2. Check your prices and the banner are still right.
3. In your Paystack dashboard, check payments match your paid orders.

## 11. When something looks wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Publish** is greyed out | Open the **Publish checklist**. Anything with an empty circle is still missing. Fix those, **save**, then publish. |
| A photo will not approve | Add the description (**Alt text**) and tick **Real photo**. For a source-bottle picture the description must say it is the original bottle. |
| "Save your changes first" | Tap **Save changes** at the bottom, then try again. |
| A size says "Sold out" on the shop | Its stock is 0, or **On sale** is off. Use **Adjust stock** or tick **On sale**. |
| A customer cannot check out | At least one **Delivery option** must be **Active**. Also check the product has a price above 0. |
| A customer says they paid but the order is "Pending payment" | Ask for their order number and open it. Within a minute or two of paying it changes by itself. If not, check the payment in your Paystack dashboard. |
| A customer lost their order number | Search for their name or phone on **Orders**, and tell them the status. |
| The admin looks out of date | Close it and open it again. |
| You forgot your password | Ask an owner to tap **Reset password** for you in **Settings**, **Team**. |

## 12. Words used in this guide

- **Decant:** a small portion of a fragrance in a travel-size bottle.
- **Draft:** saved but not on the shop yet.
- **Published:** visible to customers.
- **Flavours:** the tags that describe a scent, such as vanilla or oud.
- **Notes:** the ingredients of a fragrance in three stages: top (first), heart (middle), base (what lingers).
- **SKU:** the short code that identifies one size of one fragrance.
- **Slug:** the last part of a web address, made of lowercase words and hyphens.
- **Source bottle:** the original full-size bottle a decant is filled from.
